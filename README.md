# ⚖️ Legal Assistance Web App

This project is an AI-powered **Legal Assistance platform** built to help users — especially women — understand their legal rights, file complaints, and get quick legal support.  

The app simplifies the process of identifying laws (BNS/BNNS sections), escalating police refusals, and improving access to justice through automation and AI.

---

## 🚀 Features
- 📝 **Complaint Submission** with auto-filled IPC sections based on keywords  
- 🚔 **Police Refusal Escalation** – sends email to higher officials when a complaint is denied  
- 🌐 **AI Translation** for multiple Indian languages (via Cohere AI API)  
- 📊 **Dashboard View** – visualize complaints and status  
- 📞 **Emergency Contact Feature** – quick call for help  
- 📚 **Legal Knowledge Base** for user awareness  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Oracle APEX** (Frontend & Backend)
- **PL/SQL** (Logic & Automation)
- **ORDS / APEX_MAIL** (Email Integration)
- **Cohere AI API** (Language Translation)
- **Responsive UI** for Mobile & Web

---

## 🎯 Objective
To create a platform that promotes **women safety and legal awareness** by bridging the gap between citizens and legal authorities through AI-powered automation.
